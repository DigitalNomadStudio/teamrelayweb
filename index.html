Powerful Features

Everything you need for seamless multilingual communication

🌐

Real-Time Translation

Speak naturally and your message is instantly translated and delivered to each team member in their preferred language.

📡

100% Offline

Works completely offline using local network technology. No internet required, no data charges, complete privacy.

🎙️

Push-to-Talk

Simple push-to-talk interface for coaches. Speak clearly, and the app handles transcription and translation automatically.

🔊

Text-to-Speech

Players hear messages in their language through high-quality text-to-speech. Perfect for noisy environments.

👥

Multi-Coach Support

Multiple coaches and assistant coaches can collaborate in the same session, all communicating with players seamlessly.

🔒

Private & Secure

All communication stays on your local network. No cloud services, no data collection, complete privacy guaranteed.
